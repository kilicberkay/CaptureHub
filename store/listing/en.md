# Chrome Web Store listing: CaptureHub (English)

**Name:** CaptureHub

**Summary (132 characters max):**
Capture the visible area, a full page, a region or an element, then annotate with arrows, boxes and blur.

**Category:** Productivity

**Language:** English (default); localized name and short description in 14 more languages

**Single purpose:** Take screenshots of the current web page and annotate them.

## Description

CaptureHub takes screenshots of web pages in four ways and gives you an editor to mark them up before you copy
or save them.

Highlights

- Visible area, full page, region, and element capture; elements and regions taller than the screen are captured in full. Full page capture scrolls the page and stitches the
  pieces together, keeping fixed headers and footers in their place.
- Editor with boxes, ellipses, arrows, pen, text, pixelation, highlighter, numbered markers and crop. Seven preset
  colors, a custom color and an eyedropper, five sizes (XS to XL). Select and move any mark, undo and redo every change.
- Spot healing brush and content-aware fill remove text, icons or the cursor from a screenshot. Rotate and resize.
- Copy to the clipboard or save as PNG, JPEG or WebP.
- Shortcuts for every mode (Alt+Shift+V, F, R, E) and a right-click menu on the toolbar icon.
- Optional delay before a capture, so menus and tooltips can be opened first.
- Local history of your recent captures with the page title and site; search it, open a capture again, or show the
  downloaded file in its folder.
- Settings backup (export and import), reset, 15 interface languages, and light, dark or system theme.
- No accounts, no analytics, and nothing is uploaded.

## Permission justifications

- **activeTab:** Used when you click the toolbar icon, press a shortcut or choose a capture mode, so CaptureHub can
  capture the tab you are viewing. No access to other sites.
- **scripting:** Used to measure the page, scroll it for full page captures, and show the region and element
  selector on the page you are capturing.
- **storage:** Used to save your settings and your local capture history.
- **contextMenus:** Adds the capture modes, History and Settings to the toolbar icon's right-click menu.
- **downloads:** Saves edited captures through the browser's download manager and shows a downloaded capture in its
  folder when you ask from the history.
